| Command                              | Permission          |
|--------------------------------------|---------------------|
| `/speed [walk\|fly] <0-10>`          | basics.speed        |
| `/speed [walk\|fly] <0-10> <player>` | basics.speed.others |

> [!TIP]
> `1` is the vanilla speed and decimals such as `1.5` are allowed. Without a type, the fly speed is changed while flying, otherwise the walk speed.
